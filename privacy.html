Remy Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

The short version

Remy keeps your reminders, streaks and settings on your phone. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. Only three things leave your phone: your sign-in details, your subscription status, and what you send to Remy's AI chat if you choose to turn it on. You can delete your account inside the app at any time.

Remy is made by Diego Parra ("we", "us"). This policy took effect on October 12, 2026.

Location, Bluetooth, calendar and notification access are used on your phone to set off your reminders. They are not sent to us. If you use voice input, Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text, under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not use ads, analytics or tracking tools.

Remy's AI chat

Nothing goes to the AI until you tap Agree the first time you open the chat.

  • What is sent: the names of your reminders, how often you finish or miss them, your streak, and the messages you type.
  • Who gets it: our server passes it to Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI, which writes the reply. Anthropic handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • What we do with it: we use it only to write Remy's replies. We do not sell it or use it for ads.
  • Missed-task nudges: the notification itself is made on your phone. Only Remy's first message after you tap it uses the AI, and only if you agreed.
  • Your choice: tap Not now, or turn off "Let Remy use AI" in Profile, and nothing is sent.

AI replies can be wrong. Please don't type passwords or other sensitive details into the chat.

Who else handles your data

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, payments and the App Store.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, if you use it.
  • Supabase: runs our accounts and the server that talks to the AI.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions, using an anonymous ID.
  • Anthropic: writes AI replies, only if you agree.

These companies handle data only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. We may share information if the law requires it. We keep your account details until you delete your account. We keep the AI usage counters only as long as needed to enforce the usage limits.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: go to Profile, then Delete Account. This deletes your account on our server, wipes everything Remy saved on your phone, and tells Apple to cut the Sign in with Apple link.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Apple bills it, so cancel in iPhone Settings, then Subscriptions.
  • Turn off AI: use the "Let Remy use AI" switch in Profile.
  • Permissions: change location, Bluetooth, calendar, microphone and notifications any time in iPhone Settings, then Remy.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email us and we will help.

Kids, changes and contact

Remy is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

If we make an important change to this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
