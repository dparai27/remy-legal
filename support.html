How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Remy. You can cancel any time. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged again.

I bought Premium but it is not showing

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, then close and reopen Remy. If it still does not show, email us with the email on your Apple receipt.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In Remy, open the Profile tab and tap Delete Account. This removes your account and the data stored for it. You can also email us and we will help.

Why does Remy ask for my location, calendar or Bluetooth?

Only for the features you turn on: location reminders, Apple Calendar sync, and Bluetooth connection reminders. You can change these any time in the iPhone Settings app under Remy.
